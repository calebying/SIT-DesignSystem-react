# Masthead

The official government banner every .gov.sg digital service places at the top of the page.
Simplified from the web-component package's exact SG-crest SVG artwork to a bootstrap-icons flag
glyph -- a documented simplification, not a missing feature; the identify-toggle behaviour and
layout are otherwise faithful.
